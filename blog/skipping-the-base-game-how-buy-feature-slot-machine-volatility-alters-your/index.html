Volatility in Slot Games

Volatility, often referred to as variance, is a critical concept in slot gaming. It describes the risk level associated with a slot machine: low volatility means smaller, more frequent wins, while high volatility signals larger, less frequent payouts. Generally speaking, buy feature slots tend to exhibit higher volatility due to their inherent structure. Players are often investing significant sums upfront, hoping to maximize returns in a condensed timeframe.

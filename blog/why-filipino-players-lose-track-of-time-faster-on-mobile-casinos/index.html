This unpredictable nature keeps players on edge, leading to prolonged sessions where they forget their initial intention of merely playing for a short while. The psychological thrill of waiting for that next win can blur the lines of time perception, making it challenging for players to keep track of their game duration.

For Filipino players eager to enjoy mobile gaming while avoiding excessive time loss, implementing effective time-management strategies can be beneficial.

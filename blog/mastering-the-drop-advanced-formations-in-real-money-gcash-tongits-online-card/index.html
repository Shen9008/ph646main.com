Sacrificing Cards for Defense

By understanding the flow of the game and predicting opponents' likely actions, players can create a solid defensive posture.

For instance, if a player is holding two high cards and one low card, they might discard the low card even if it’s a non-risky play. By doing this, they ensure that they retain the potential to win with their strong cards, forcing opponents to rethink their strategies as they try to guess the high cards held.
